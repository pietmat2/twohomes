Everyday life

A quieter week, in both homes.

Many Danish children have two homes. Small things keep the week running: the swimming kit, the dentist, who picks up on Friday. Twohomes keeps them in one place, so they need less discussion.

How it works

Three steps to get started.

  1. 1

    Set the pattern

    Pick the rotation you already live by and the date it starts. The calendar fills itself in for every month ahead, holidays included.

  2. 2

    Invite the other home, when it suits you

    Both homes can sync, end-to-end encrypted, so a request lands on the other phone. A read-only link to next month is also an option. No account needed.

  3. 3

    Then it runs quietly

    A reminder the evening before every changeover, with that child's bag list. Costs added as they happen. A clear history for both homes.

Practical details

Good to know.

Using it on your own

Twohomes works for one parent from day one. The other home can join whenever it suits, and nothing stops working if they do not.

Privacy

Your information is visible to you, and to the other home if you pair with them. What passes between the homes is encrypted end-to-end, so the relay in the middle cannot read it, and neither can we.

Cost

Free during the private beta. You would hear about any change first. One parent's access never depends on the other's payment.

Your existing agreement

Pick the rotation that matches it, or set your own. Any single day can still be changed by hand.

Who it is for

Parents who mostly manage day to day. It has no evidence logs or court exports. For a dispute, Familieretshuset or a family lawyer can help.

Backups

Your data stays on your device. You can save an encrypted copy whenever you like, and take it with you if you leave.

Phones and languages

iOS, Android and the web, in English, Danish and Polish.

About

Made with the children in mind.

Twohomes is for parents living apart who would like the practical side of the week to take less effort. Every feature is judged by whether it makes the week quieter for the children.

It is not made for court or for high-conflict cases. It is for two parents who mostly manage, and who would like the admin to take thirty seconds instead of an evening.

  • The child is the user. Decisions are settled by what makes their week calmer.
  • Local-first. Your data lives on your device. Sharing is a choice you make, screen by screen.
  • Neutral by design. No read receipts, no scoreboard, no nudges.
  • Quiet on purpose. Calendars and ledgers should be plain, fast and quiet.
